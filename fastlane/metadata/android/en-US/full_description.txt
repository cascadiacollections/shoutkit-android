ShoutKit is a lightweight internet radio player. Search the community-run
Radio Browser directory (radio-browser.info), save your favorite stations and
listen in the background.

Features:
• Search and browse stations by name and genre, or add a station by its stream URL
• Background playback with notification, lock screen and Quick Settings tile controls
• Shows the current song when a station sends track information
• Favorites, recently played stations and recently heard songs
• Import and export your saved stations
• Sleep timer and equalizer presets (Normal, Bass Boost, Vocal, Treble)
• Pauses when headphones disconnect and reconnects after stream errors
• Home screen widget and app shortcuts
• Android Auto and Android TV support
• Play links (sir://play/…) for routines, Tasker and home screen shortcuts

Network services:
• Station search uses the Radio Browser API. By default ShoutKit tells Radio
  Browser when you start one of its stations, so popular stations rank higher.
  You can turn this off in Settings.
• Optional album artwork sends the current song's artist and title to Apple's
  iTunes Search. It is on by default and can be turned off in Settings.

Privacy:
• No account or sign-in
• Preferences are stored on your device
• This build contains no Google Play Services, Firebase, analytics or crash
  reporting. Chromecast and the Wear OS companion app are only in the Google
  Play build.
